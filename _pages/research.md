## Research Direction

My research centers on physics-based simulation and computer graphics, with a focus on robust contact, constraint-based dynamics, rod simulation, cutting, and real-time interactive systems. I am especially interested in algorithms that make simulation more accurate, controllable, and practical for complex physical interactions.

## Current Work

### Thickness-Aware Rod Insertion Simulation

I am developing accurate rod-insertion simulation algorithms based on Projective Dynamics, Cosserat rods, and Lagrangian constraint-based methods. The project focuses on modelling thickness-aware rod contact pressure and rotational friction, with the goal of handling challenging insertion and puncturing scenarios more faithfully.

Status: manuscript in preparation, 2026.

## Previous Research

### Neural and Particle Flow Maps for Fluid Simulation

During a summer research internship at Georgia Institute of Technology with Dr Bo Zhu, I replicated the Neural Flow Map algorithm in Taichi and developed a real-time 3D stable fluid simulation using OpenGL Compute Shader. I also worked on accelerating Particle Flow Map with long-range mapping and short-range projection, reaching an 80% speed improvement.

As a part-time research assistant at City University of Hong Kong with Dr Zhicong Lu, I studied games, players, and well-being. ### Hand-Scratching Gesture Recognition for AR Glasses

With Dr Kening Zhu at City University of Hong Kong, I developed an Android application that used CNN models to recognize scratching gestures on the leg of AR glasses from audio in real time, then used the recognition result to control the glasses.

- Physics-based simulation: Projective Dynamics, FEM, Cosserat rods, soft-body contact, cutting, low-rank updates.
