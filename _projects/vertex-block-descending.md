This project focuses on the optimization of Vertex Block Descending for simulation systems. The work studies solver behavior, performance, and practical integration with physics-based simulation pipelines.

Research focus:

- Vertex-level block optimization.
- Solver performance and convergence behavior.
- Integration with deformation and contact simulation.
- Manuscript submitted in 2026.
