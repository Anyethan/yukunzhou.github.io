During a summer research internship at Georgia Institute of Technology, advised by Dr Bo Zhu, I worked on neural and particle flow map methods for fluid simulation.

Contributions:

- Replicated the Neural Flow Map algorithm using Taichi.
- Developed real-time 3D stable fluid simulation with OpenGL Compute Shader.
- Accelerated Particle Flow Map with long-range mapping and short-range projection.
- Achieved an 80% speed improvement in the accelerated Particle Flow Map pipeline.
