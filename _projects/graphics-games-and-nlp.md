This page collects selected implementation-heavy projects from computer graphics, game development, simulation, and machine learning.

## Physics-Based Simulation Implementations

Implemented mass-spring systems, keyframe interpolation with quaternion and Euler approaches, inverse kinematics with pseudo-inverse methods, and Tikhonov regularization in C++.

## Unity Game Development

Led, designed, and developed Unity 3D game projects including Lucidmare, Omnis, and Donuts!, with Donuts! published on Steam.

## 3D Human Rendering

Built a 3D graphics course project with URP, hair anisotropy, and skin separable subsurface scattering under Unity. I also learned and deployed skin separable subsurface scattering in GLSL.

## NLP for Finance

Advised by Dr Linqi Song at City University of Hong Kong, I developed a BERT model in PyTorch to analyze sentiment in large-scale financial comments.
