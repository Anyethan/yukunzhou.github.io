This project developed a hand-scratching gesture recognition Android application for AR glasses, advised by Dr Kening Zhu at City University of Hong Kong.

Technical work:

- Used CNN models to classify scratching sounds on the leg of AR glasses.
- Built real-time audio recording and recognition on Android.
- Connected recognition results to AR glasses control.
