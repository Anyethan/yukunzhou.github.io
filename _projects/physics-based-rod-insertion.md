This project studies accurate rod-insertion simulation using Projective Dynamics, Cosserat rods, and Lagrangian constraint-based methods. The main focus is modelling thickness-aware rod contact pressure and rotational friction for insertion and puncturing scenarios where standard contact approximations are not enough.

Key components:

- Rod dynamics with Cosserat rod modelling.
- Thickness-aware contact pressure.
- Rotational friction for insertion behavior.
- Projective Dynamics and constraint-based solver design.
- Experiments and manuscript preparation.
