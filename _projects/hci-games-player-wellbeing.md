As a part-time research assistant at City University of Hong Kong, advised by Dr Zhicong Lu, I worked on an HCI research project about games, players, and well-being.

Project scope:

- Studied how games and ICT4D applications can support player well-being.
- Developed design implications and future application scenarios.
- Produced a manuscript submitted to a top-tier conference as the only first author.
